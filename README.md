# Kakei - Personal Finance Manager CLI
Kakei (家計) is a CLI to manage your personal finance holding everything on your PC and using your favorita LLM to help you understand and plan better. Inspired by hledger, this is crafted to match my personal needs as a guy who NEEDS to control the finances but struggles to use the mainstream apps. The main problem is that as an app that focus on controlling your expenses, they focus too much on selling me something that most the times was not worth it. This aims to be simple, direct and easy to use. First starting with a CLI, but with plans to create a PWA, mobile app and even a self-hosted ecossystem. Take control of what is yours.

## Technology used
- Go
- SQLite
- charmbracelet/bubbles
