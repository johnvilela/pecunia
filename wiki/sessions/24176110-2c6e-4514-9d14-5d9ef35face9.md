## What happened

Implemented [[tasks/05-installments-and-credit-card-bill]] end-to-end via TDD: card bills generated on read, bill payments as ordinary account transactions, and multi-installment card purchases. Full design write-up: [[decisions/0009-bills-as-rows-and-installments-as-transactions]].

### Build order

- Schema first: `internal/db/migrations/006_card_bills.sql` (`card_bills` table), pinned by `TestCardBillsSchema` — one bill per card per closing date, status CHECK, cascade delete with the card, and deleting a bill unlinks (not deletes) any transaction that paid it.
- `cards.AddMonths` added and tested (`TestAddMonths`) — fixes the drift plain `time.AddDate(0,n,0)` causes on month-end dates (31 Jan + 1 month → 28/29 Feb, not 3 Mar); the clamp is per-step from the original day, not cumulative.
- New `internal/bills` package (`bill.go`, `store.go`, `ui.go`), each written red-then-green.
- `internal/transactions` extended: `SplitInstallments` (remainder rides the first share), `Validate` rules (only a card purchase can be split; only an account outcome can pay a bill; a card can't pay its own bill; an income can't pay a bill), `IsInstallment`, `PayBill`, and `ScopeOne`/`ScopeForward`/`ScopeAll` added to `Update`/`Delete`.
- `cmd/cards.go` gained `showBills`/`showBill`/`payBill`, wiring `kakei cc bill` and `kakei cc pay`.
- `scripts/seed/main.go`: added a 5x installment fixture ("Celular novo", R$1000 on NUCRD) and `seedBillPayment`, which pays two-fifths of NUCRD's oldest unpaid bill from INTER so the dev DB shows every bill status (open/closed/partial/paid) at once. Also swapped a stale seed income fixture from "payment against the invoice" framing to a refund, since paying a bill is now `kakei cc pay`, not a plain card income.

### Bug caught live

Reseeding and driving the dev DB (`./dev cc bill`, `./dev cc bill NUCRD 2026-07`) surfaced: an *open* bill's running total was rendered as money "left" and the bill was offered by `kakei cc pay` — wrong, since an open cycle is still taking charges, not a debt yet. Fixed by splitting `Bill.Owed()` (zero while open, equal to `Remaining()` once closed) out from `Remaining()`, and making `Store.Unpaid`/`OldestUnpaid` filter on `Owed() > 0` instead of `Remaining() > 0`. Full rule: the "`Owed()` is not `Remaining()`" section of [[decisions/0009-bills-as-rows-and-installments-as-transactions]].
